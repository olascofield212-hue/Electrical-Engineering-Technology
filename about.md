# About Electrical Engineering Technology

Electrical engineering technology is an applied technical field that connects engineering principles with hands-on implementation. It focuses on how electrical, electronic, automation, control, and power systems are installed, tested, operated, maintained, and improved.

## What electrical engineering technologists do

Electrical engineering technologists frequently work with engineers, electricians, technicians, project managers, operators, and skilled tradespeople. Depending on the workplace, their responsibilities may include:

- interpreting schematics, wiring diagrams, specifications, and technical manuals;
- assembling, testing, commissioning, and troubleshooting equipment;
- using multimeters, oscilloscopes, insulation testers, process instruments, and diagnostic software;
- supporting programmable logic controllers, motor controls, sensors, and industrial networks;
- preparing technical reports, maintenance records, drawings, estimates, and project documentation; and
- applying quality, regulatory, and workplace safety requirements.

## Engineering technology and engineering

Both fields use mathematics, science, and problem solving, but they often emphasize different parts of the project lifecycle.

| Electrical engineering | Electrical engineering technology |
| --- | --- |
| Often emphasizes analysis, theory, modelling, and original system design. | Often emphasizes application, implementation, testing, operation, and technical support. |
| May develop design calculations and system architecture. | May translate designs into working equipment and verify performance in the field or laboratory. |
| Commonly prepares for professional engineering pathways. | Commonly prepares for technologist, testing, maintenance, automation, and project-support pathways. |

The boundaries can overlap, and actual responsibilities depend on education, experience, employer requirements, and provincial or professional regulations.

## Major areas of study

### Electrical circuits and power

Students learn circuit laws, alternating and direct current, transformers, motors, protection, distribution, and energy use.

### Electronics

Electronics courses introduce semiconductor devices, analog and digital circuits, power electronics, embedded systems, and electronic testing.

### Automation and control

Automation combines programmable logic controllers, sensors, human-machine interfaces, variable-frequency drives, industrial communication, and process control.

### Instrumentation and measurement

Technologists must select instruments, obtain reliable measurements, interpret results, calibrate equipment, and document findings.

### Technical communication and project work

Modern technical roles also require clear reports, drawings, procedures, presentations, teamwork, scheduling, and version-controlled documentation.

:::caution Electrical safety

Electrical systems can cause severe injury, fire, arc-flash exposure, and equipment damage. Never perform electrical work beyond your training or authorization. Follow applicable codes, lockout/tagout procedures, manufacturer instructions, and qualified supervision.

:::

## Industries and workplaces

Electrical engineering technology skills are used in manufacturing, public transportation, utilities, construction, consulting, renewable energy, telecommunications, building automation, mining, aerospace, and equipment service organizations.
