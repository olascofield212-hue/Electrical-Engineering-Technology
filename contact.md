## Professional and educational resources

### IEEE

The [Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers](https://www.ieee.org/) provides technical publications, standards, educational resources, professional communities, and information about developments in electrical and electronic engineering.

### OACETT

The [Ontario Association of Certified Engineering Technicians and Technologists](https://www.oacett.org/) supports engineering technicians and technologists in Ontario and provides information about professional certification, ethics, continuing development, and career recognition.

### Electrical Safety Authority

The [Electrical Safety Authority](https://esasafe.com/) provides electrical safety information for Ontario, including public guidance, licensing information, and resources related to electrical installations.

### Ontario College of Trades and Skilled Trades Ontario

[Skilled Trades Ontario](https://www.skilledtradesontario.ca/) provides information about apprenticeships, trade certification, and regulated skilled trades in Ontario.

## Suggested learning pathway

1. Review electrical safety fundamentals and laboratory procedures.
2. Strengthen mathematics, circuit analysis, and measurement skills.
3. Practise interpreting schematics and technical documentation.
4. Build small projects involving sensors, relays, motors, or controllers.
5. Document results with drawings, test records, photographs, and concise reports.
6. Seek supervised laboratory, co-op, apprenticeship, or entry-level field experience.
